## RPCS3 evidence

### v0.1.11 screen

After installation and launch, RPCS3 visibly rendered the user-supplied background and white `PRESS START TO EXIT` text. Blue bands, stripes, and `DISPLAY ACTIVE` were absent. The app reached normal rendering. This verifies emulator pixels only; it does not prove physical PS3 HDMI output or controller exit.

### v0.1.12 startup

The fresh log reached:

- `NET_IP_ROUTE_READY`
- `CRYPTO_READY`
- `RTSP_LISTEN_READY value=5001`
- `RTP_AUDIO_READY value=6000`
- `RTP_CONTROL_READY value=6001`
- `RTP_TIMING_READY value=6002`
- `MDNS_FAILED value=4 bytes=112`
- `AUDIO_READY`
- diagnostic tone start and completion

The public header maps stage 4 to UDP bind. In the PS3DK POSIX errno header, 112 is `EADDRINUSE`. A read-only host check found a UDP listener on port 5353; a temporary wildcard UDP bind probe failed with `EADDRINUSE` even after setting `SO_REUSEADDR`. This strongly supports a local RPCS3 host-port conflict. The test did not establish whether the same conflict would occur on the physical PS3.

The emulator log does not prove the tone was audible. No mDNS service readiness, iPhone discovery, RTSP session, RTP stream, ALAC decode, PCM playback, or Spotify audio was observed. RPCS3 was stopped through its Emulation menu after capture. Its prior Internet setting (`Disconnected`) was restored; PSN remained disconnected.
