## Decision record

- **Decision:** Treat this as a strong improvement signal, not audio acceptance.

- **Why this option was chosen:** Current evidence narrows the likely issue but does not isolate remaining artifacts; a same-source longer sample avoids speculative tuning.
- **Tradeoffs accepted:** Hardware evidence is recorded without shipping another build yet.
