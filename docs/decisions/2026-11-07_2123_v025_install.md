## Decision record

### Leave the new app running during the soak

- **Decision:** Collect the log after 30 minutes without sending controls to the console during playback.
- **Context:** The requested test needs uninterrupted AirPlay behavior and a longer trace than v0.2.4 allowed.
- **Options considered:** Inspect or stop the app mid-run, or wait until the requested window ends.
- **Why this option was chosen:** It preserves the user's test and avoids interrupting streaming.
- **Tradeoffs accepted:** No intermediate diagnosis is available until the soak ends.
